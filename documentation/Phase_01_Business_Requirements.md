# Phase 1 — Business Requirements (HealthMart)

## Overview
HealthMart is a fictional healthcare retail company used to simulate a real-world analytics engineering environment.

---

## Business Problem
The company currently lacks a centralized analytics system. Data is spread across multiple operational systems.

---

## Goal
Build a modern analytics platform that enables:

- Revenue tracking
- Customer analytics
- Inventory monitoring
- Marketing performance analysis

---

## Core Business Processes

### 1. Sales
Customer purchases across stores and online platforms.

### 2. Inventory
Tracks product stock levels and movement.

### 3. Returns
Tracks refunded or returned products.

### 4. Marketing
Tracks campaigns, promotions, and coupons.

---

## Stakeholders

- CEO → Revenue & growth
- COO → Operations & inventory
- CFO → Financial performance
- CMO → Marketing effectiveness

---

## KPIs

- Revenue
- Orders
- Customer lifetime value
- Stock availability
- Campaign performance

---

## Outcome of Phase 1
We now understand:

- What the business does
- What systems exist
- What questions need to be answered

This will guide all future data modeling decisions.
